About Us

We believe students have the potential to become creators, innovators and entrepreneurs. Our goal is to help students explore their ideas, develop practical projects and discover opportunities while studying in Canada.

Encouraging Student Innovation

The CIP Innovation & Incubation Hub aims to provide a supportive environment where students can explore entrepreneurial ideas, collaborate on projects, learn from industry experience and take their concepts towards practical solutions.
