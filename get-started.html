HOW TO GET STARTED

From Your Idea to a Working Product

The process can be adapted to the needs and stage of each project.

01

Share Your Idea

Describe the problem, who it affects and the solution you have in mind.

02

Discuss the Opportunity

Talk through the idea, its users, feasibility and possible next steps.

03

Plan and Build

Explore a practical development plan and guidance from relevant industry experts.

04

Test and Improve

Develop an early version, gather feedback and improve the product.

INDUSTRY GUIDANCE

Build with the Right Guidance

Depending on the project and available support, students may be connected with industry experts for feedback on product direction, technical choices, user needs and next steps.

The goal is to help you move from an idea towards a useful product as efficiently as possible. Timelines and outcomes depend on the scope and readiness of each project.
