More Than Education. A Platform for Innovation.

The CIP Innovation & Incubation Hub is a joint initiative by [Person 1] and [Person 2], launched as an extension of the services offered through CIP Study Abroad.

Through regular innovation meetings, mentorship, project development support and funding opportunities, we help students take their first steps towards entrepreneurship and innovation.
