Meet the hub

More Than International Education. A Platform to Innovate.

The CIP Innovation & Incubation Hub is a joint initiative by Ravi and Gautam, created as an extension of the services offered through CIP Study Abroad.

Our aim is to help students make the most of their international experience by exploring ideas, developing practical projects, connecting with mentors and learning about entrepreneurship while studying abroad.

Students can bring their own ideas or collaborate on project ideas introduced through the hub. Together, they can explore challenges, build solutions and learn by doing.
