Why Build While Studying in Canada?

Use your time in Canada to understand local needs, build practical experience and create solutions that can make a difference.

🇨🇦

Understand and Contribute to Canada's Economy

Learn how Canadian industries and communities work, identify needs and explore ways your ideas or products could contribute.

🤝

Build Solutions for Newcomer and Immigrant Needs

Use your own experiences and community insights to explore practical solutions to challenges faced by newcomers and immigrant communities.

🧩

Build Canadian Project Experience

Work on a real project, develop practical skills and create a portfolio that demonstrates what you can do.

📈

Strengthen Your Post-Graduation Career Prospects

Develop relevant experience, relationships and examples of your work that may help you stand out when pursuing opportunities after graduation.

Project participation does not guarantee employment, immigration outcomes or any particular career result. Opportunities depend on individual circumstances and eligibility.
