Why Build While Studying in Canada?

Your international student experience can be more than a qualification. It can be a starting point for understanding challenges, creating solutions and building a professional portfolio.

📈
01 / MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Contribute to Canada's Economy

Understand Canadian markets and industries, identify unmet needs and explore how a useful product or service could contribute to the local economy.

🤝
02 / REAL-LIFE CHALLENGES

Address Newcomer Challenges

Use lived experience and community insight to explore practical solutions for challenges international students, newcomers and immigrant communities may face.

🛠️
03 / PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE

Build Canadian Project Experience

Work through a real problem, collaborate with others and develop a project you can demonstrate through a portfolio or product showcase.

🎓
04 / CAREER READINESS

Strengthen Post-Graduation Prospects

Showcase initiative, problem-solving, teamwork and practical skills that may strengthen your professional profile when pursuing opportunities after graduation.

The hub does not provide immigration or legal advice. Participation does not guarantee employment, work authorization, permanent residence or any immigration outcome.
